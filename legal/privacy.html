1. Who is responsible for your data

The controller of your personal data is the operator of Browser Stock Exchange (browsermarket.online), a sole operator based in the United States.

Browser Stock Exchange is run by one person rather than a company, so there is no company number to give. Full registered contact details are held on file and are provided on request to a supervisory authority, or to anyone with a legitimate legal need for them. Write to the address below and they will be sent to you.

Contact us about privacy at support@browsermarket.online.

  • No contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.
  • No special category data under GDPR Article 9. Please do not put any into a display name or a support email.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

We do not set analytics cookies of our own. The Service uses the local storage listed above, which is strictly necessary to provide a game you asked to play and to keep you signed in. Advertising cookies are set by Google, not by us; see section 7.

Google and its partners set their own cookies and identifiers to serve and measure ads. Where consent is required for those, Google's own consent message asks for it before any such cookie is set, and you can change your answer at any time through that message.

6. Email you get from us

6.1 Account email

We send email that is necessary to operate your account: sign-in codes, purchase receipts, security notices, and notices about material changes to the Terms or this policy. You cannot opt out of these while you have an account, because without them the account cannot work.

6.2 Marketing email

  • Marketing email is off unless you turn it on. The box is not pre-ticked.
  • It covers product news, new features and offers. Nothing else.
  • You can turn it off at any time in Settings → Account → Product email, by using the unsubscribe link in any marketing email, or by emailing support@browsermarket.online.
  • Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of anything we sent before you withdrew it.
  • We do not sell or rent your email address, and we do not use it to advertise anybody else's product.

7. Advertising

The Service may show advertising, including rewarded placements you choose to watch. Where an ad network is connected, it may process an advertising identifier, your IP address and general device information to select and measure ads. Those third parties are independent controllers for that processing and their own privacy policies apply.

We do not share your email address, your account identifier or your game save with advertisers. We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising as those terms are defined in US state privacy laws.

Our advertising partner is Google. Third party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this and other sites on the internet.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by Google at Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of a third party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices or, in Europe, youronlinechoices.eu.
  • How Google uses data from sites that use its services is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Rewarded placements in this game are ads you choose to watch in exchange for something in the game. Nothing in the game is gated behind watching one that cannot also be reached another way.

We keep the purchase record described in 2.3 to grant what you bought, to handle refunds and disputes, and to meet tax and accounting obligations.

12. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can:

  • See what we hold. Ask for a copy of your personal data.
  • Correct it. Ask us to fix anything inaccurate.
  • Delete it. Delete your account in Settings, or ask us. We will delete it unless we must keep something for a legal reason, in which case we will tell you which and why.
  • Take it with you. Ask for your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Change your mind about marketing. At any time, in one click.
  • Complain. To us first, please, and to a regulator if we do not put it right.

Email support@browsermarket.online to exercise any of these. We respond within 30 days, and will tell you if we need longer. We do not charge for this and we will not treat you differently for asking. We may need to verify that the request comes from you, normally by emailing the address on the account.

15. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. In territories where a higher digital age of consent applies, that age applies instead.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us personal data, contact support@browsermarket.online and we will delete the account and its data promptly.

16. Security

We take measures appropriate to the risk, including:

  • encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest;
  • no passwords to steal: sign-in is a one-time code sent to your address;
  • row level security on every table, so a database row can only be read by the account it belongs to;
  • publishable API keys scoped so that they grant no access to anyone else's data;
  • least-privilege access for the small number of people who can reach production;
  • rate limits on sign-in and other sensitive actions.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach is likely to result in a high risk to your rights and freedoms, we will tell you without undue delay, and notify the relevant regulator within 72 hours where required.

17. Automated decisions

We do not carry out automated decision-making that produces legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affects you, and we do not profile you for advertising.

We do use automated rules to detect cheating and abuse, which can lead to an account being suspended. If that happens to you, email support@browsermarket.online and a person will review it.
